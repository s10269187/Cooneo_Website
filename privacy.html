Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information COONEO Innovation Technology (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. ("COONEO", "we", "us") collects when you use this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

This website is run by COONEO Innovation Technology (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. If you have any questions about this policy or your personal information, email us at cooneo@outlook.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address. Your password is stored securely by Google Firebase — we cannot see it. If you use "Continue with Google", we receive your name and email address from Google.
  • Delivery details: first and last name, phone number, address, postal code, city and country.
  • Orders: the products, options and quantities you order, your order notes and the language you used.
  • Cart and favourites: the products you save, so they are still there next time you log in.
  • Website usage: pages visited, approximate location (country or city), device and browser type, and how you arrived at our website, collected through Google Analytics cookies.
  • On your device only: your language choice (English / 中文) is saved in your browser.

3. How we use your information

  • To process your orders and contact you about pricing, payment and delivery.
  • To send you order confirmation emails.
  • To keep your account, cart, favourites and order history.
  • To answer your questions and requests.
  • To understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with the service providers that run this website for us:

  • Google Firebase — accounts, login and storing orders.
  • Google Analytics — visitor statistics.
  • EmailJS — sending order emails.

We may also share information if required by law.

5. Where your information is stored

Your information is stored on the servers of the providers above, which may be located outside your country. By using this website, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in other countries.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. Order records are kept for [TO CONFIRM – e.g. 5 years] for business and accounting purposes. After that, we delete them or make them anonymous.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your personal information, or to close your account. You can update your delivery details yourself under My Account. For anything else, email cooneo@outlook.com and we will reply within [TO CONFIRM – e.g. 30 days].

8. Cookies

We use Google Analytics cookies to count visitors and see which pages are popular. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the website will still work.

9. Security

This website uses a secure connection (HTTPS), and your account and orders are protected by Google Firebase's security. No website is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.

  • 购物车和收藏：您保存的产品，方便您下次登录时继续查看。

3. 我们如何使用您的信息

  • 处理您的订单，并就价格、付款和发货与您联系。
  • 向您发送订单确认邮件。
  • 保存您的账户、购物车、收藏和订单记录。

我们不会出售您的个人信息。我们只会与为本网站提供服务的以下服务商共享：

  • Google Firebase：账户、登录及订单存储。
  • Google Analytics：访客统计。
  • EmailJS：发送订单邮件。

如法律要求，我们也可能依法提供相关信息。

5. 信息存储地点

您的信息存储在上述服务商的服务器上，这些服务器可能位于您所在国家/地区以外。使用本网站即表示您理解您的信息可能会被传输到其他国家/地区并在当地处理。
